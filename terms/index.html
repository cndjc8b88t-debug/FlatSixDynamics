The Short Version

FlatSixDynamics is a real-time computational fluid dynamics demonstration that visualises the airflow around a 2D cross-section of a sports car. It exists to be looked at, learned from, and enjoyed. It is not an engineering tool. Treat the numbers it shows the way you would treat the figures on a wristwatch's tachymeter scale: meaningful within the instrument, not authoritative outside it.

What the App Is

FlatSixDynamics solves the compressible Navier–Stokes equations in two dimensions over a discretised grid using textbook numerical methods (HLLC flux, MUSCL reconstruction, SSP-RK3 time integration). It displays the resulting velocity, pressure, and derived quantities as a heatmap, vector field, and live telemetry.

It is intended for: appreciation of aerodynamic phenomena, educational exploration, and the simple pleasure of watching pressure waves form behind a moving object.

The App is not:

Numerical values shown in the App are illustrative. They are derived from a simplified two-dimensional model running on consumer-grade GPU hardware in real time, with all the approximations that implies. Do not use them to inform any decision that has consequences in the physical world.
